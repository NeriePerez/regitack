Account Management Guidelines

Recommended account management practices

  • Assign only the role required by the user's responsibilities.
  • Use a temporary password of at least eight characters.
  • Deactivate accounts instead of deleting historical records.
  • Keep at least one active administrator account.
  • Password changes and account updates are recorded in the audit log.
